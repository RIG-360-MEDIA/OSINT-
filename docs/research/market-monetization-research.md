## 1) TOP 10 features people DO pay for (ranked, with proof)

1. **Cited, grounded synthesis over a trusted corpus** — *Perplexity ($20/mo).* Trust = source quality + faithful citations.
2. **Search over a premium/private corpus you can't read manually** — *AlphaSense / Hebbia / Factiva ($2.6k–$125k/yr).* The corpus is the moat.
3. **Real-time, first-alert event detection** — *Dataminr / Recorded Future ($40k–$500k/yr).* "Know first" is worth the most money in the market.
4. **AI feeds + entity/topic monitoring with alerts** — *Feedly Threat/Market Intel ($1.6–3.2k/mo).* Persistent monitoring, not one-off queries.
5. **Bias / blindspot / multi-perspective framing** — *Ground News (~$30/yr).* Demonstrated consumer willingness on a thin feature.
6. **Deep-research agents that return cited answers at scale** — *Hebbia / Elicit / Undermind ($10–42/mo → $10k/seat).*
7. **Grounded Q&A over MY documents + audio overview** — *NotebookLM ($19.99/mo).* Audio is the sleeper payable format.
8. **Structured extraction into tables** — *Elicit / Hebbia.* "Answer 50 questions across 500 docs into a grid."
9. **Personalization + cross-session memory** — *Perplexity Pro/Max.* The assistant that *knows your beat.*
10. **Network / terminal lock-in + workflow integration (Slack/Teams/API/SSO)** — *Bloomberg, Glean, Feedly Enterprise.* Distribution into where work happens.

## 2) TOP 10 still-poorly-solved pain points (gaps = your opportunity)

1. **Article-level (not outlet-level) bias/framing** — Ground News stops at the outlet; nuance is missing. You have per-article stances/claims → **article-level bias/framing is an open gap.**
2. **Multilingual cross-language story stitching (EN/HI/TE)** — incumbents are US/English-centric; nobody clusters an English wire story with its Hindi/Telugu coverage and contrasts framing. **Your core moat.**
3. **Trust collapse from open-web spam citations** — Perplexity cites AI-spam blogs. A **curated, source-graded corpus** is defensible.
4. **Indian/regional OSINT intelligence at a sane price** — Dataminr/RF/AlphaSense are $100k+ and West-centric; **no "Palantir-lite for the India desk."**
5. **Entity/person/place dossiers over time** — "everything on X across articles+clips+clippings+social, with a timeline + stance shift." Fragmented today.
6. **Verifiable claim/quote provenance** — who said what, when, where, did it change. Your claims/quotes/stances graph directly serves this.
7. **Real-time alerts you actually trust** (low false-positive, entity-scoped) — Dataminr is noisy + costly; precision alerting for a *beat* is underserved.
8. **Personalized audio brief of YOUR feed** — NotebookLM does docs, not a live personalized news feed. **Daily multilingual audio brief = sticky + payable.**
9. **Affordable mid-market intelligence tier** — huge gap between $20/mo consumer and $100k/yr enterprise. **The $50–500/mo prosumer/team band is wide open.**
10. **Local-language vernacular monitoring** — vernacular media is invisible to Western tools; comms/PR/political/policy buyers in India badly need it and will expense it.

## 3) Pricing model recommendation (Indian-multilingual-news intelligence)

**Core principle:** Indian *consumer* WTP for news is low (~17–18% pay globally; lower in India; 71% of non-payers won't convert at any flex price — Reuters DNR 2025). **Do not bet the company on consumer subscriptions.** Make consumer free/cheap for funnel + data; **monetize prosumer/B2B who expense tools** (journalists, analysts, comms/PR, political/policy, corporate-affairs, academics, NGOs).

| Tier | Audience | Includes | INR price | USD ~ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Mass / top-of-funnel | Personalized feed, multi-perspective story view, basic semantic search, 1 daily text brief, limited agent queries/day | ₹0 (ad-light) | — |
| **Pro (prosumer)** | Journalists, researchers, analysts, prosumers | Unlimited agent RAG + live-web, **article-level bias + cross-language framing**, entity dossiers + timelines, claim/quote provenance, **daily personalized audio brief**, saved alerts, export/citations | **₹799–₹1,499/mo** (₹7,999–₹14,999/yr) | ~$10–18/mo |
| **Team / Desk** | Newsrooms, PR/comms desks, policy shops (3–15 seats) | Shared watchlists/boards, **entity + topic alerts (precision real-time)**, collaboration, API-lite, Slack/Teams/WhatsApp delivery, role-based access | **₹4,999–₹14,999/seat/mo** or **₹2.5–8 L/yr** per desk | ~$60–180/seat/mo |
| **Enterprise / Intelligence** | Govt, corporate-affairs, large media, risk teams | Full OSINT firehose, custom entity graphs, on-prem/VPC, SSO/SAML, audit, SLA, custom sources, dedicated support | **Custom, ₹15 L–₹1 Cr+/yr** | $18k–$120k+/yr |

**Add-on levers** (proven elsewhere):
- **Premium-content packs** (licensed publishers / paywalled clippings) as a **+₹199/mo** add-on — Comet Plus model.
- **Usage-metered agent/deep-research credits** above a tier cap — Hebbia/Elicit model; protects margins.
- **Audio brief as the wedge** to convert Free→Pro (NotebookLM proves audio is payable; nobody does it for a live Indian multilingual feed).
- **Annual-only discount + 7-day Pro trial**; avoid auto-renew friction (a top Ground News complaint).

**Founder takeaways:**
- The corpus + cross-language clustering + claims/stances graph **is the moat** — features (report-gen, summaries, chat) are commoditized (STORM is free).
- Sell **"know first + know fairly + in every language"** to people who get paid to know — not to consumers who won't pay for news.
- Land at **prosumer ₹999/mo**, expand to **desk/team**, monetize **enterprise OSINT** for the cash; keep consumer free as a data/distribution flywheel.
