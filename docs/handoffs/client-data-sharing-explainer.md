# How RIG Shares Data With You — Client Explainer

*(Plain-language, client-facing. Hand this over or read from it.)*

## What you get
Your own secure window into RIG's intelligence, **scoped to you** — your entities, your districts, your topics. We do the collecting, translating, and analysis across English **and** regional-language news, YouTube, and newspapers; you get the finished intelligence.

You can consume it **two ways** (pick one or both):
- **The dashboard** — a live, branded web desk (ROBIN-OSINT) showing your situation brief, sentiment, map, dossiers, and "ask" assistant.
- **A secure API** — pull the same data straight into *your* systems/dashboards.

**What the data is:** not just raw articles, but structured intelligence — entities, **directed sentiment** (who's supportive vs critical toward you), topics, claims, quotes, locations, story clusters, daily briefs, and trend analytics.

## How it stays *your* data — and only yours
- **You are an isolated tenant.** You only ever see your own slice. You can **never** reach another client's data — that's enforced on our servers by design, not by trust.
- **Access is tied to your login / API key.** Nothing you send can widen what you're allowed to see.
- **Every figure is auditable** — traceable to the exact source article.

## Always live
It updates continuously. You get the current picture, not a one-time export that's stale by tomorrow.

## What we don't do (so expectations are clear)
- We share **structured intelligence + snippets + source links**, not wholesale copies of other publishers' full articles (copyright).
- We can scope strictly to your watchlist so nothing off-topic appears.

## Commercial shape
We run and maintain the whole platform; you get the intelligence on a **subscription**, metered, and revocable at any time. No infrastructure for you to build.

## To set it up, we need to know:
1. Do you want the **dashboard**, the **API**, or **both**?
2. Do you need data to **live inside your own systems**, or just to **access** ours?
3. Any **data-residency / compliance** rule we must meet?
